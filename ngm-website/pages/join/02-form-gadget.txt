NATIVE WA GADGET — "Membership application": REMOVE IT FROM THE JOIN PAGE
=========================================================================
The Join page (slug /Join-Duplicate) no longer embeds the membership form.
The form lives on its own page — /application — and the hero in 01-top.html
carries a "Start the membership application" button that links there.

Why: when the form was embedded on Join, the later steps (name/address,
payment, confirmation) also rendered in-page, surrounded by the marketing
sections — confusing mid-application. A dedicated form page keeps every
step clean.

In the WA page editor for the Join page:
1. DELETE the "Membership application" gadget that sits between the two
   Custom-HTML gadgets (01-top and 03-bottom).
2. If the layout row that held it had a white background, delete the row
   too (or clear the background) so 01-top's cream hero flows straight
   into 03-bottom's linen pricing section.

The /application page needs no changes — it already exists and is skinned
by the site-wide MEMBERSHIP APPLICATION blocks in global.css (see
system-pages/membership-application/).
