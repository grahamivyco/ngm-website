Other ways to give

Prefer to give another way?

The form above is the quickest way to give online. If you’d rather send a check, give monthly, or honor someone special, here’s how.

By mail

Prefer a check? Mail it to the Needlework Guild of Minnesota, PO Box 16506, St. Louis Park, MN 55416.

Give monthly

Set up a recurring monthly gift in the form above to support the Guild all year long.

In honor or memory

Dedicate a gift to a stitcher you love — note the honoree with your donation and we’ll record the dedication.

Thank you

Every gift counts

However you give, your support keeps the needle arts alive in Minnesota. Thank you for being part of it.
