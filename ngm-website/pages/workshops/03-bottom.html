What to expect

Learn from the best

Workshops range from half-day sessions to multi-day intensives, taught by Guild members and visiting instructors alike. They run throughout the year — usually in person in Bloomington, with some offered on Zoom — and most are open to members and non-members alike. Kit fees vary by class and are paid with registration, so watch the calendar for what's coming up.

Where we meet
