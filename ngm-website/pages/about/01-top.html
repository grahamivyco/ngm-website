Our beginnings

Over five decades of stitching

The Guild began in 1972, after a statewide needlework exhibition drew far more stitchers than anyone expected. Minnesota, it turned out, was full of people who loved handwork — and wanted to keep meeting.

That shared enthusiasm grew into the warm, welcoming community we are today.

More than fifty years later, the same spirit carries on — in monthly meetings, workshops, the annual retreat, and the friendships stitched along the way.
