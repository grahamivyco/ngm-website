A Fall Finish

November 7 & 8, 2026 · Plymouth, MN · $41/dayMembers & guests

Almost as fun as starting a project is finishing it! Following our spring “March to the Finish,” we’re gathering again with fellow stitchers to enjoy our love of handiwork — and maybe finish those holiday gifts. Come for one day or both.

About the day

A day to finish

Spend the day stitching alongside fellow Guild members, working toward the finish line on projects big and small. Each day there’s a drawing for two gift certificates, plus a free table — bring anything you’d like to give away. Just remember: whatever isn’t claimed by the time you leave goes home with you.

  • Open stitching
  • Gift-certificate drawings
  • Free giveaway table
  • Catered lunch
The details

What to expect

When

Saturday & Sunday, November 7–8, 2026. Times to be announced — come for one day or both.

Where

Plymouth Comfort Inn, 3000 Harbor Lane North, Plymouth, MN 55447. Phone 1-763-299-0101.

Cost

$41 for one day or $82 for both days. A catered lunch is included — note any gluten-free, lactose-free, or onion-free needs when you register.

Register by

Registration is open September 1–29, 2026. Space is limited to 40 people each day, so sign up early.

Stay over

Optional — a block of sleeping rooms is available at $100 per night plus tax. You’ll need to make your own reservation with the hotel.

Free table

Bring items to give away if you like. Anything not taken by another stitcher before you leave comes back home with you.

Catered lunch

On the menu

Choose one item per day when you register. Sandwiches and wraps are served with kettle chips, fruit, and a cookie; salads are served with bread and butter.

Sandwiches

On ciabatta, with kettle chips, fruit, and a cookie.

  • Roast beef

Served with kettle chips, fruit, and a cookie.

  • Chicken Caesar

Salads

Served with bread and butter.

  • Chicken Caesar

The hotel provides coffee, decaf, hot water for tea, and ice water; the caterer provides assorted regular and diet sodas at lunch. You’re welcome to bring a snack for yourself as well.
