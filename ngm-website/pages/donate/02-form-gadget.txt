NATIVE WA GADGET — "Donation form"
===================================
Add the native WA "Donation form" gadget DIRECTLY on the Donate page, BETWEEN
01-top.html and 03-bottom.html, in the SAME layout column. Putting it here (not
on WA's separate /donation page) is what makes the form show inline — visitors
don't click "Donate now" and get bounced to another page. The hero "Donate now"
button just scrolls down to this gadget (#ngm-give).

1. Add gadget -> "Donation form".
2. Point it at the Guild's donation settings (one-time + recurring).
3. Set the layout ROW background to linen (#F0EAE0).

No CSS class is needed: the form is skinned by the "DONATION FORM" section of
global.css. That skin is scoped to body.ngm-donate-page (added by 01-top.html),
so it works no matter what wrapper class WA emits. The linen band runs
continuously from the "Give online" heading in 01-top, through the form, into
03-bottom.
