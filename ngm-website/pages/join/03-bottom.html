Membership

Simple, annual dues

$30
per year — about $2.50 a month

The membership year runs June 1 through May 31. Joining sets up your account, then you’ll pay your dues securely online — and your first visit to any meeting is always free. Dues cover all monthly meetings and Special Interest Groups, The Needle’s Eye newsletter, workshops with professional instructors, and the circulating library.

What you get

Your annual membership includes

The Needle's Eye newsletter

Our bi-monthly newsletter with updates on upcoming programs and activities.

All monthly meetings

Participate in any or all of the Guild's monthly Stitch-Ins and Special Interest Groups.

Educational materials

Access to program materials prepared by fellow members for monthly meetings.

Workshops with pro instructors

Enroll in workshops covering a diverse array of techniques for all skill levels.

The circulating library

Borrow from a growing collection of books, periodicals, and DVDs.

Anyone with an interest in needlework is welcome — no experience needed. Come & Stitch and Sunday Tea & Stitch are open to everyone with no membership required.
