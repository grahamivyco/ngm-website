# Updating the Bylaws & Procedures page

For whoever keeps the Guild's governing documents current. No code, and
nothing to install. Everything happens inside the Wild Apricot admin site.

**You'll need:** admin access to the website, and the document saved as a PDF.

---

## How the page is put together

Under the page title there's one box holding a few **groups**:
*Governing documents*, *Policies* and *Waivers*.

Each group is just two things: a **heading line** and a **bulleted list**
under it. One document per bullet. That's the whole format.

---

## Replacing a document with a new version

For example, the treasury policies have been revised.

### 1. Put the PDF on the website

**Website → Files**, open the **Documents** folder, and upload the PDF.
Put the date in the name so old and new are easy to tell apart:

```
NGM Treasury Policies 2026-10.pdf
```

### 2. Open the page

**Website → Site pages → Bylaws and Procedures**, then **Edit**.

### 3. Re-point the link

1. Click into the box, then click on the document's name.
2. Click the **link** button in the toolbar.
3. Pick the new PDF from step 1. Click OK.

### 4. Update the date

The date comes after a dash on the same line:

```
Treasury policies — Oct 2026
```

Change the text after the dash. It shows as a small label beside the name.

### 5. Save

Click the document in **Public view** to check it opens the new PDF.

---

## Adding a new document

1. Upload the PDF (step 1 above).
2. Open the page and click into the box.
3. Click at the end of the last bullet in the right group and press
   **Enter** to get a new bullet.
4. Type the name, then a dash and the date if it has one:
   `Membership policy — Jan 2027`
5. Select the whole line, click the **link** button, and pick the PDF.
6. Save.

**Always link it.** A bullet with no link shows as a greyed-out row with a
"Not posted" label.

## Removing a document

Click into the bullet, select the whole line, and delete it. Save.

## Adding a new group

1. Click at the end of the last bullet on the page and press **Enter**
   twice to leave the list.
2. Type the group's name on its own line, e.g. `Forms`.
3. Press **Enter** and click the **bulleted list** button in the toolbar.
4. Add the documents as above.
5. Save.

## Please don't

- **Don't put anything else in the box.** Headings and lists only. No
  intro text or notes. The wording at the top of the page is already
  handled.
- **Don't retype an entry to move it.** Retyping loses its link. Cut the
  line and paste it where you want it. The link comes with it.
- **Don't touch the box at the very top** (the title and the "Back to the
  Member Hub" button). That part is set up separately.

## Something looks wrong?

**The list shows plain bullets and blue underlined links.** The box's
**CSS class** has been cleared. Select the box, open its settings, and
check the **CSS class** field says `ngm-wa-docs`.

Anything else, email the website team at admin@needleworkguildmn.org
rather than trying to fix the layout by hand.
