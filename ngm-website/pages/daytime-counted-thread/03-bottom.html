What we explore

Counted techniques, every month

We meet the 2nd Tuesday of every month, 10 am to 2 pm, in person in Bloomington or by video. Each meeting centers on a program that alternates between counted thread and needlepoint — but you’re always welcome to bring your own project instead, and every skill level is welcome.

Visit

Where we meet

This group meets at Minnesota Valley Unitarian Universalist Fellowship (MVUUF) in Bloomington, and on Zoom. Your first visit is always free — you don’t have to be a member to come and stitch with us.
